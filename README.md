# The Library — TMoC Books

**Discovering books. Bridging civilisations.**

[Visit The Library](https://tmoc-89.github.io/tmoc-books/)

The Library is an Infrared Community initiative that brings previously untranslated books from around the world into modern, readable English. Its purpose is to make works of history, philosophy, politics and culture accessible across languages, freely and without a subscription.

The collection brings together English translations and original texts in Arabic, French, German and Chinese. Where an original is available, readers can switch between it and the translation, or compare passages in Reviewer Mode. Longer works have dedicated volume readers, with contents that follow the structure of each book.

## Read, compare and take part

- Read on a phone, tablet or desktop, with adjustable text size and light or dark themes.
- Find a passage through the contents or search across the collection.
- Return to your saved reading position.
- Download complete books as Markdown for reading offline.
- Help improve a translation: mark passages, suggest wording, add notes and export a review file for the editors.

The translations are prepared by TMoC and other community members using frontier AI models. The Library treats translation as a shared, continuing task: original texts and review tools help readers examine the wording and contribute corrections. Individual edition notes record source choices, editorial decisions and known limits.

The latest addition is Wang Huning’s *America Against America* (*美国反对美国*), presented in English alongside the Chinese original. The collection also includes works by Mullā Ṣadrā, Henry Corbin, Husayn Muruwwa, Hadi al-Alawi, Carl Schmitt and others.

Every work is free to read. Explore the [collection](https://tmoc-89.github.io/tmoc-books/#collection), follow an idea through [search](https://tmoc-89.github.io/tmoc-books/search/), or join the review of a passage that matters to you.
