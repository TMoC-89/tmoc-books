№ 07 · Four-volume work · From the Arabic

Materialist Tendencies in Arab-Islamic Philosophy

النزعات المادية في الفلسفة العربية - الإسلامية

Husayn Muruwwa · حسين مروة

A sweeping alternative history of Arab-Islamic philosophy, from pre-Islamic Arabia through Ibn Sīnā, organised around a Marxist reading of social history, intellectual conflict and the development of philosophical thought. All four volumes are freely available in modern English alongside the Arabic text.
