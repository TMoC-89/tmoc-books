Materialist Tendencies in Arab-Islamic Philosophy

النزعات المادية في الفلسفة العربية - الإسلامية
Husayn Muruwwa · حسين مروة

A sweeping alternative history of Arab-Islamic philosophy, from pre-Islamic Arabia through Ibn Sīnā, organized around a Marxist reading of social history, intellectual conflict, and the development of philosophical thought. This edition makes all four volumes freely available in modern English alongside the Arabic text.

Why the work is split into four reading pages

Each volume has its own reader, chapter navigation, reading progress bar, language switch, and Markdown downloads. A persistent I–IV switch keeps the whole set connected. This avoids turning the largest work in the Library into one unwieldy page, while preserving the sense that it is one continuous book.
