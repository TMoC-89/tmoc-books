№ 10 · Four-volume work · From the French

Within Iranian Islam

Spiritual and Philosophical Aspects

En Islam iranien · Aspects spirituels et philosophiques

Henry Corbin · Gallimard, 1971–1972

Henry Corbin’s masterwork on the spiritual and philosophical life of Iran after Islam: Twelver Shiism and its hermeneutics, Sohrawardî’s philosophy of light, Rûzbehân and the Sufism of the “faithful of love”, the School of Isfahan, the Shaykhi School and the Twelfth Imam. The four volumes have never had a complete English translation; here they are in modern English, with the complete French original alongside.

Four volumes, one work

Read in order, or start where you need

  • Volume I

    Twelver Shiism

    Le shî’isme duodécimain

    Shiism and Iran, the Twelve Imams, the sacred book and its inner meanings, prophecy and walâyat, and what the Imam means for Shiite spiritual life.

    English · Français · About 10 hours
  • Volume II

    Sohrawardî and the Platonists of Persia

    Sohrawardî et les Platoniciens de Perse

    Sohrawardî’s “Eastern” theosophy of light: the Zoroastrian Light of Glory, the archangelic hierarchies, the Grail, and the tales of the Crimson Archangel and the Western Exile.

    English · Français · About 11 hours
  • Volume III

    The Faithful of Love · Shiism and Sufism

    Les Fidèles d’amour · Shî’isme et soufisme

    Rûzbehân Baqlî of Shiraz and the Sufism of the faithful of love; then Haydar Âmolî, Semnânî and others on where Shiism and Sufism meet.

    English · Français · About 11 hours
  • Volume IV

    The School of Isfahan · The Shaykhi School · The Twelfth Imam

    L’École d’Ispahan · L’École shaykhie · Le Douzième Imâm

    Mîr Dâmâd, Mollâ Sadrâ and Qâzî Sa’îd Qommî; Shaykh Ahmad Ahsâ’î and his successors; the Twelfth Imam and spiritual chivalry.

    English · Français · About 14 hours
