Transcribed Arabic

While enemy forces occupy our land, while rearmament and reconstruction demand all our energy, and while we face the challenge of catching up with the modern world, the cry has begun to rise again from more than one direction:

Wolf! Wolf!

While the enemy occupies our land, and the troubles of armament and reconstruction need all our energies, and the challenge of catching up with the age faces us... there rose again, once more, and from more than one place, the cry: The wolf! The wolf!!

بينما العدو يحتل أرضنا، ومتاعب التسليح والتعمير تحتاج إلى كل طاقاتنا، وتحدي اللحاق بالعصر يواجهنا.. عادت ترتفع مرة أخرى، ومن أكثر من مكان، صيحة:
الذئب! الذئب!!

Once again we are being frightened with a monster called atheism, unleashed upon the world by an even greater monster which some call Marxism and others socialism.

The frightening with a ghoul whose name is atheism has returned, let loose upon the world by its ghoul-mother... which, in the opinion of some, is (Marxism), and in the opinion of others (socialism).

عاد التخويف من غول اسمه الإلحاد، أطلقته على الدنيا أمه الغولة .. التي هي في رأي البعض (الماركسية) وفي رأي البعض الآخر (الاشتراكية).

So what is the real issue?

So what is the story exactly?

فما هي الحكاية بالضبط؟

I hesitated before writing about Marxism and atheism because I believed there were more urgent matters demanding our attention.

Our country bears the wounds of war. We are trying to build a future under external threats and in the middle of serious economic difficulties.

And the truth is that I hesitated before writing around this subject about Marxism and atheism. And I used to imagine that there are other subjects more urgent and more entitled to attention. For our countries are heavily wounded with the wounds of wars, and they look forward to building the future among external threats and amid economic difficulties.

والحقيقة أنني ترددت قبل الكتابة حول هذا الموضوع عن الماركسية والإلحاد. وكنت أتصور أن هناك موضوعات أخرى أكثر إلحاحاً وأحق بالاهتمام. فبلادنا مثخنة بجراح الحروب، وهي تتطلع إلى بناء المستقبل بين تهديدات خارجية ووسط مصاعب اقتصادية.

Those concrete problems, I believed—and still believe—deserve our concentration, our debate, and even our disagreements.

But some people have succeeded in forcing the question of belief and unbelief back onto the agenda. They insist that it comes before everything else. And because they identify Marxism with atheism, and claim that Marxism in particular is the threat to our religious values, the matter has to be clarified.

And these real problems are the ones that deserve the concentration of effort and dialogue and disagreement. I used to imagine this, and I still do. But it appears that some succeed in inserting the question of atheism and faith into the agenda, and some see that discussing this question comes before any other matter. And since this opinion declares that atheism equals Marxism—indeed, it sees that the threat to our religious values comes only from Marxism in particular—then it has become necessary to clarify the truth.

وهذه المشاكل الواقعية هي التي تستحق تركيز الجهد والحوار والخلاف، كنت أتصور هذا، ولا زلت. ولكن يبدو إن البعض ينجح في إقحام قضية الإلحاد والإيمان في جدول الأعمال، ويرى البعض أن مناقشة هذه القضية يأتي قبل أي مسألة أخرى، وبما أن هذا الرأي يعلن أن الإلحاد يساوي الماركسية، بل وهو يرى أن ـ التهديد لقيمنا الدينية لا يأتي إلا من الماركسية بالذات، وإذن فقد وجب توضيح الحقيقة.
