The Four Journeys of the Intellect

The Transcendent Wisdom of the Four Intellectual Journeys

الحكمة المتعالية في الأسفار العقلية الأربعة

Mulla Sadra · صدر الدين محمد الشيرازي · Nine-volume bilingual edition

Mulla Sadra’s great philosophical work, The Transcendent Wisdom in the Four Intellectual Journeys, traces the path from the questions of existence and causation to the nature of God, the world, the soul and the afterlife. Read all nine volumes in English or switch to the original Arabic at any point. Every volume has its own table of contents and endnotes; the complete bilingual Markdown is available to download.
