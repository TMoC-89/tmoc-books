    A shared undertaking

    Discovering books.
    Bridging civilisations.

    The Library is an Infrared Community initiative to make previously untranslated books accessible to English readers.

    Where available, the original text sits alongside the translation: switch between them while reading, or compare them passage by passage in Reviewer Mode. Every work is free to read, and each can be downloaded as Markdown for reading offline.

    Translated by TMoC and other community members using frontier AI models.
